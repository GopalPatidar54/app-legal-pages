Summary & Commitment to Your Privacy

Sumora: Number Logic Puzzle does not require user accounts, passwords, or personal registrations. This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Sumora: Number Logic Puzzle ("the App"), developed and maintained by the developer ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you download, install, and play our game.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We respect your personal privacy. We do not collect or store:

  • Your name, home address, physical location, or phone number.
  • Your personal email address (unless you voluntarily contact our support team).

  • Biometric data, contacts, camera feeds, microphones, or photos.

3. Information Stored Locally On Your Device

The App operates primarily as an offline-capable client. To provide gameplay functionality, the App uses your device's local storage (via React Native AsyncStorage) to save:

  • Campaign Progress: Current level reached, unlocked levels, stars earned (1-3 stars), high scores, and best moves per level.
  • Daily Challenge Progress: Daily puzzle completion history, current streak, best streak, and in-progress daily game board state.
  • Game Preferences: Audio settings (BGM volume, voice feedback, sound effects), active visual theme (Classic, Underwater, Nature), and tutorial intro completion status.
  • Hint & Inventory State: Remaining free hints and rewarded hints earned.

This data is stored purely locally on your device. It is not transmitted to our private servers and can be wiped at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device's Settings or by uninstalling the App.

Google AdMob (Google LLC)

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Device Specifications: Device model, operating system version, screen size, language, carrier, and network connection type.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, impressions, and engagement metrics.
  • IP Address: Used strictly for approximate regional ad delivery and fraud detection.

For details on how Google processes and protects advertising data, please review:
Google Privacy Policy • How Google uses information from apps

5. How to Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising

You can control or reset your advertising identifier and opt out of personalized (interest-based) advertising directly through your mobile device settings:

  • On Android: Open Settings → Google → Ads and enable "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization".
  • On iOS: Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

Note: Opting out does not reduce the number of ads shown, but ads will be generic and non-targeted.

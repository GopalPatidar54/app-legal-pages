1. Introduction

Welcome to All-in-One File Tools ("we," "our," or "the App"), developed for fast, secure on-device file manipulation. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application across Android and supported platforms.

By using All-in-One File Tools, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. What Data We Process & How It Is Used

A. User Files and Content (100% Local Processing)

The core functions of the App include image compression, video compression, PDF optimization, PDF conversion (Image to PDF, PDF to Image), PDF merging, and PDF page extraction. For all these features:

  • Local Native Codecs: All compression and conversions are executed directly on your device utilizing built-in operating system hardware codecs (such as Android MediaCodec / iOS AVFoundation) and local native rendering modules.
  • No Cloud Uploads: Your images, videos, audio, and documents are never transmitted to external servers, cloud storage, or third-party APIs.
  • Temporary Processing Cache: Any temporary working files generated during processing are saved in your application-isolated temporary directory and can be cleared at any time via the App's built-in "Clean Cache" feature.

B. Device Permissions

To enable file selection and saving, the App requests standard operating system permissions:

  • Storage / Media Permissions (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required only so you can browse, select, and view the files you wish to compress, convert, or organize.
  • Write Permissions (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on compatible OS versions): Required solely to save the resulting processed files, images, or PDFs to your public device storage (e.g., Downloads, Documents, or Gallery).
  • Media Location (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION): Used only so that when you process photos or videos that already contain location or capture metadata (EXIF / MediaStore), the App can preserve that authentic metadata on the saved or replaced file. The App does not track your live GPS location in the background.
  • Internet: Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad delivery and connectivity checks. Core file tools work offline.

These permissions are used strictly to perform the actions you explicitly trigger.

C. Local Preferences and State

The App saves basic configuration and operational values locally on your device using AsyncStorage:

  • User interface theme preference (Light / Dark mode).
  • Selected display language (English or हिन्दी).
  • Default save-mode preference and related tool toggles.
  • Virtual reward points balance.
  • Local processing history (for example, tool name, source/output filenames, local file URIs, sizes, and timestamps). This history never leaves your device.

This preference and history data remains exclusively on your device and is not synchronized to external accounts. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the App removes it.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To keep the application free for everyone, the App integrates third-party advertising services provided by Google. Your personal media files are not sent to Google for processing. Advertising-related device data may still be transmitted by the ad SDK.

Google AdMob & Google Play Services

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to serve banner advertisements, native advanced ads, and opt-in rewarded video ads. Ads may be personalized (interest-based) unless you opt out in your device or Google Ads settings. Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID on Android / IDFA on iOS).
  • IP address (used to estimate approximate coarse location for localized ad delivery).
  • Device model, operating system version, and general diagnostic signals for ad fraud detection.
  • Ad interaction metrics (views, clicks, rewarded video completion events).

For more information on how Google collects and protects your information, please review the official Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Technologies.

You can manage or reset personalized ads through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads) or by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Virtual Reward Points System

All-in-One File Tools features an optional virtual reward points mechanism that enables extra batch processing capacity:

  • Reward points have zero monetary or cash value.
  • Points cannot be exchanged, transferred, redeemed, or refunded for real-world currency or physical goods.
  • Points are earned exclusively inside the App by voluntarily watching rewarded video advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Your point balance is stored locally on your device and resets if you clear application data or uninstall the App.

5. Data Security

Because file compression and conversion occur directly on your physical hardware, your personal media files never leave your device for processing. For advertising data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, transmission uses industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption as provided by Google.
